Learn AI Productivity with Phoebe

Personal ops is the work around the work: deciding what to read, answering, chasing what you are owed, tracking what you owe, and checking once a week that nothing slipped. It repeats, so it is the part of a job most worth handing to software, and it fails silently, so it is the part where handing it over badly costs the most. Six sessions build one system for it and measure every step: where your week goes, how good a triage rule really is against a base rate, what an assistant is actually allowed to do in your mailbox, and the weekly review that keeps all of it honest.

The six sessions 🎯 · from a five-day log to a system you review

The running artifact is your own week: your log, your rules, your open-loops list, your scopes, your review. The bench uses 390 real messages from three mailboxes in the public Enron email corpus, each labelled by what its owner actually did with it.

What is honest here 🧾

The bench is real public data, counted in your browser. Every statistic on the pages is quoted from the document it came from, with its scope; where a famous number is narrower than its reputation, the page says how.
